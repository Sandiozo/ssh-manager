# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome to use, discuss and contribute to SSH Manager, whatever their background, experience
or identity. We want this project to be friendly, respectful and free of harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and on what is best for its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, spam, or any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't
follow this code of conduct, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or contact the
maintainer through a [private report](https://github.com/Sandiozo/ssh-manager/security/advisories/new).
Reports are handled confidentially.
